# DevLedger Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 29 October 2026*

DevLedger is an Android app built for The Independent Institute of Education (IIE) PROG7314 Portfolio of Evidence. This policy explains what the app stores and why.

## What we collect
- **Account details:** your name and email address, or the name and email returned by Google or GitHub when you use single sign-on.
- **Content you create:** code snippets, folders, tags, financial transactions, categories, receipts and other attachments, and your app settings (language, base currency, monthly budget, sync choices).
- **Notification token:** a Firebase Cloud Messaging token so the server can send you budget alerts.

## What stays on your phone only
- **Your PIN.** It is stored as a salted hash in encrypted storage and is never sent to our servers.
- **Biometric data.** Android handles fingerprint and face recognition. DevLedger only receives "success" or "failure" and never sees biometric data.

## How it is used
Your data is used only to run the app: to sign you in, to keep your vault and ledger in sync between your phone and your account, and to send alerts you can switch off in Android settings. We do not sell your data and do not show advertising.

## Where it is stored
Data is kept in a PostgreSQL database behind the DevLedger API, hosted on Render. All traffic uses HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes.

## Sharing
Data is shared only with the services needed to run the app: Google and GitHub (sign-in) and Google Firebase (push delivery).

## Your choices
You can change the language, sync behaviour, budget and notification permission at any time in the app or in Android settings. To access or delete your data, email REPLACE_WITH_CONTACT_EMAIL.

## Children
DevLedger is intended for adults and students aged 18 and over.

## Changes
We will update this page and the date above if this policy changes.
